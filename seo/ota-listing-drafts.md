## 1. The Chef's Table (pasta making class)

**Title** (68 characters)
Florence: Pasta Making Class with Ravioli, Tiramisù and Wine (Max 8)

Alternative (68): Florence: Small-Group Fresh Pasta and Tiramisù Class in the Oltrarno

**Short description**
Make fettuccine, ravioli and your own tiramisù by hand with two Tuscan chefs in our Oltrarno
kitchen, then sit down to eat it all with a glass of wine. Never more than 8 guests.

**Full description**

Three hands-on hours in our kitchen in Florence's Oltrarno, near Piazza Santo Spirito. You mix,
knead and roll your own dough with a chef at your elbow, cut fettuccine into ribbons by hand,
fill and seal ravioli with no air trapped inside, and make your own tiramisù. No demonstrations
watched from a stool: it is hands in the flour from the first minute.

You pick the sauce for your fettuccine (tomato and basil, cacio e pepe or amatriciana) and the
kitchen cooks it while you work, so the three hours stay on the pasta. The ravioli go with butter
and sage. Time and season permitting, the chefs also show you how the same dough becomes the
Tuscan cuts, hand-rolled pici and wide pappardelle, so you can make them at home.

Most Florence classes call twelve people a small group. Ours never seats more than eight, which
is the whole reason a chef is beside you when a fold goes wrong. When the pasta is done, everyone
sits down together to eat what they made: your fettuccine, your ravioli and your tiramisù, with a
glass of Tuscan wine (or a soft drink), then limoncello or coffee. All included in the price, with
no upsell at the end.

Your hosts are Endri Cerhozi and Marsel, two lifelong friends who are the head chefs of two
agriturismi in the hills outside Florence. Pasta for weddings, feasts and live shows is their day
job; this kitchen is where they teach it.

Afterwards we email you the recipes for everything you made, and whatever you don't eat is dried
and bagged so you can take it back to your hotel. Classes run every day, including Mondays, in
English or Italian. The 18:00 slot runs the same class in the evening and finishes into dinner.

**Highlights**
1. Make fettuccine, ravioli and your own tiramisù entirely by hand
2. Never more than 8 guests, so a chef is always at your elbow
3. Taught by two head chefs of Tuscan agriturismi
4. Sit down to eat everything you made, with a glass of Tuscan wine included
5. Take home the recipes by email and any pasta you don't finish

**Includes**
- Hands-on pasta making class, about 3 hours, theory and practice
- English-speaking chef (class in English or Italian)
- All fresh ingredients (seasonal Tuscan products)
- Your own workspace, apron, knife and rolling pin
- The meal you made: fettuccine with your choice of sauce (tomato and basil, cacio e pepe or amatriciana), ravioli with butter and sage, your own tiramisù
- 1 glass of wine or a non-alcoholic drink
- 1 glass of limoncello or a coffee
- Water
- Recipes for everything you made, by email
- Leftover pasta, dried and bagged to take away

**Excludes**
- Making the sauce (the kitchen cooks it while you work on the pasta)
- Extra food and drinks

**Meeting point**
{{OWNER: exact address}}
Our kitchen in the Oltrarno, near Piazza Santo Spirito, five minutes' walk from the Ponte Vecchio.
The class ends at the same place.

**Important information**

Dietary (as stated on the site):
- Gluten-free: a dedicated gluten-free flour blend, your own station, board and tools, and your
  own pot of cooking water, at no extra charge. Tell us when you book. Wheat flour is used in the
  same room, so this is not a certified gluten-free kitchen.
- Vegetarian: no changes and no surcharge. Choose tomato and basil or cacio e pepe and the whole
  menu is vegetarian. Only the amatriciana contains meat.
- Vegan: available with advance notice, no surcharge. Eggless dough, tomato and basil or aglione
  sauce, ravioli with a potato filling, olive oil and sage, and an adapted dessert. Egg dough,
  cheese and butter are used at the same table, so the kitchen itself is not vegan.
- Other allergies: tell us when you book.

Children (from the family class page, same class):
- Ages 6 and up cook with us; every child needs an adult at the table.
- €50 for ages 6 to 12; under 6 eat free but do not cook. Adults €95.
- Children get the same meal without the wine or limoncello.
- Knife work is limited and supervised; boiling water stays with the chefs.

Experience: none needed. Everything is taught from zero, and most guests have never made fresh
pasta before.

Accessibility: not stated on the site. Leave the platform's accessibility fields empty until the
owner confirms them.

**Cancellation** (summary of handmadepastaflorence.com/cancellation-policy/)
- More than 48 hours before the class: free cancellation, deposit refunded in full.
- Less than 48 hours before, or a no-show: the deposit is non-refundable and the full class price
  may be charged.
- Free date change up to 24 hours before, subject to availability. After that, a change request
  counts as a cancellation under the timeline above.
- Cancellations count from when we receive the message (WhatsApp or email).
- If we have to cancel (illness, an emergency, or the class not reaching its minimum group size),
  you choose a free reschedule or a full refund.
- Refunds go back the way you paid (bank transfer, or card via Stripe) once the cancellation is
  confirmed; card refunds can take a few days.

## 2. Mercato & Mani (market tour and cooking class)

**Title** (67 characters)
Florence: Sant'Ambrogio Market Tour and Pasta Cooking Class (Max 6)

Alternative (65): Florence: Shop the Market with a Chef, Then Cook Ravioli and Ragù

**Short description**
Shop Sant'Ambrogio, the market where Florentines actually shop, with a Tuscan chef, then cook the
basket into fresh ravioli, a slow ragù and a seasonal dessert in our Oltrarno kitchen. Max 6 guests.

**Full description**

Start the day the way our chefs do: at Sant'Ambrogio market, tasting and choosing what looks best.
It is smaller and quieter than the Mercato Centrale, and full of the stallholders our chefs buy
from. You walk it with Endri or Marsel, learn what to look for in this week's produce, and build
the menu from what is good that morning. There is no fixed shopping list: the season decides.

Then the basket goes back to our kitchen in the Oltrarno and becomes lunch. You make fresh egg
dough and fold it into ravioli, start a ragù and let it cook while you work, and finish with a
seasonal dolce. With never more than six guests, this is the closest thing we offer to cooking
side by side with a chef at home.

Like every class we run, it ends at the table: your pasta, your ragù, your dessert and a glass of
local wine.

Your hosts are Endri Cerhozi and Marsel, head chefs of two agriturismi in the hills outside
Florence. This is our longest class and our smallest group, about five hours in all, and it tends
to book out first. Taught in English or Italian.

**Highlights**
1. Shop Sant'Ambrogio, the Florentines' own market, with a Tuscan chef
2. Build the menu from what is in season that morning, with no fixed shopping list
3. Make fresh egg-dough ravioli, a slow ragù and a seasonal dessert
4. Never more than 6 guests, our smallest group
5. Finish at the table with what you made and a glass of local wine

**Includes**
- Guided walk through Sant'Ambrogio market with Endri or Marsel
- Hands-on cooking class in our Oltrarno kitchen (about 5 hours in total, market included)
- English-speaking chef (English or Italian)
- Fresh ingredients
- Your own workspace, apron, knife and rolling pin
- The meal you cooked: ravioli, ragù and a seasonal dessert
- A glass of local wine

**Excludes**
- Extra food and drinks

**Meeting point**
{{OWNER: exact address}}
At Sant'Ambrogio market. The site does not give the exact spot. The class ends at our kitchen in
the Oltrarno, near Piazza Santo Spirito ({{OWNER: exact address}}).

**Important information**
- Wear comfortable shoes: the market walk is part of the class.
- The site describes this class as better suited to teenagers than toddlers. No minimum age is
  stated.
- Gluten-free and allergy-friendly at no extra charge: a dedicated gluten-free flour blend and a
  clean station. Tell us about any allergies when you book.
- Vegetarian or vegan versions are not stated for this class (the ragù is part of the menu).
  Leave this out until the owner confirms.
- Accessibility: nothing stated beyond the walking. Leave the platform's accessibility fields
  empty until the owner confirms them.

**Cancellation**
Same policy as The Chef's Table above (48 hours free cancellation, free date change up to 24
hours before, full refund or reschedule if we cancel). Link: handmadepastaflorence.com/cancellation-policy/

  Chef's Table and "dawn" for the market.
