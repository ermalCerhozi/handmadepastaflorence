## 1. The cheese tasting (this is the urgent one)

There is a cheese experience in Florence sold on Viator called "Masterclass on
Cheese in Florence: Enjoy a Whole Cheese Board". **It is no longer bookable.** We
checked.

People are still searching for it by name, in English and in German. Google is
already showing them our site for the German version of that exact title, at the top
of page two, and we don't have a cheese page at all. So there is a group of people
looking for something they can't buy, and we are already nearly in front of them.

To build the page we need:

1. **Which cheeses, and from which producers?** Name, milk, roughly how old, and the
   farm or village if you can. What do you serve with them, honey, jam, bread?
2. **Is it an add-on to The Chef's Table, a tasting on its own, or both?**
3. **Price per person.**
4. **How long, how many guests at most, and which days and times?**
5. **Wine pairing, or not?**
6. **Any limits for vegetarians, pregnant guests or lactose intolerance?** Which
   cheeses use animal rennet or raw milk.

## 2. Groups of 15 or more

Unchanged since October, and the numbers got worse. Companies looking for bigger
groups found us 675 times in the last six weeks and clicked **zero** times. The page
says "6 to 14", they read it and leave.

1. **What is the largest group you can take?**
2. **Where do groups over 14 cook?** The Oltrarno kitchen tops out at 14, so the
   page has to name somewhere real.
3. **How should we show the price?** Per person or per group, and is there a minimum?
4. **Do you also take private groups**, birthdays and family reunions, or companies
   only? Most people searching "group cooking class" are friends and families.

## 3. Reviews

This is the one that would help most, and it costs you nothing to write.

Every competitor that outranks us shows a star rating in Google results. We show
none, because we have no reviews on the site. The code is already built and waiting:
the moment real reviews go in, the stars appear in Google automatically.

**Can you forward any real reviews guests have left you?** Google, Tripadvisor,
WhatsApp messages, anything where we can see who wrote it. We will not invent any,
and we will not put up a rating we can't stand behind.
